# Nordic News Summarizer

Deliver concise, insightful digests of recent news from Norway and Finland.

## Core Workflow

1. Search recent (24-48h) news sources for Norway (NRK, VG, etc.) and Finland (Yle, Helsingin Sanomat).
2. Select 5-8 most interesting, unusual, or high-impact stories.
3. For each:
   - One-sentence headline + summary
   - Why it matters / potential impact
   - Context if needed
4. Group by theme if relevant (politics, tech, society, weather, economy).
5. End with brief overall takeaway for the period.
6. Cite sources where possible.

## Output Format

- **Story 1**: [Headline]  
  Summary. Impact: ...

Prioritize originality and significance over volume.

## Tips

- Prefer primary or reputable outlets.
- Note if stories involve EU, NATO, energy, or local politics.
- For Norway: oil, welfare, nature, immigration often relevant.
- For Finland: tech, education, Russia relations, sauna culture side stories.
